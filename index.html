The idea

One field, one river,
one fire.

Larkspur buys from eleven farms inside thirty miles, one oyster bed across the river, and nothing that flies. The menu has four courses and no exceptions section, because a kitchen that commits can cook for anyone: tell us what you cannot eat, and the plates change around you, not the restaurant.

Everything touches the hearth. The bread, the birds, the beets, the butter. Embers at five hundred degrees cook differently than any pan on any stove, and that difference is the whole restaurant.

The room

A barn the river built

A 1904 apple barn on Front Street, moved stone by stone in 2019. Forty-seven seats, one hearth, and a window wall that looks straight down the Hudson to Breakneck Ridge.
