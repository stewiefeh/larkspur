02

Type

Fraunces speaks, Inter works. One variable file each, self-hosted, five clamp() steps that scale with the reader's own font size.

--text-hero / clamp(3.2rem, 2rem + 6.5vw, 7.5rem) / 0.94 line

Eat what the valley is having.

--text-2xl / clamp(2rem, 1.5rem + 2.4vw, 3.25rem)

One field, one river, one fire.

--text-lg / display weight 480

Section heading, fourth scale

--text-md / body, 1.75 line, 62ch measure

Body text runs at the reading measure cooks use for recipes: long enough to think in, short enough to follow at a counter with flour on your hands.

--text-xs / 0.22em tracking / uppercase

The eyebrow, smallest voice in the room

03

Motion

Three timing curves, one stagger system, and a hard promise: everything collapses to instant states under prefers-reduced-motion.

The wipe
300ms / scaleX
The spring
ease with overshoot
The tilt
perspective, 8deg
Entrances

One observer, staggered children

IntersectionObserver with a 15% threshold and an 8% bottom margin. Children take a --reveal-delay of 80ms per index, capped at 240ms so nothing waits on nothing.

Scroll-driven

CSS animation-timeline, enhanced only

Parallax figures and grow bars ship behind @supports (animation-timeline: view()) and disappear gracefully where the browser says no.

Reduced motion

Instant, not absent

Reveals render in their final state, the marquee slows to a crawl instead of stopping, and the scroll cue goes still. Motion is a preference, not a dependency.

05

The rules

Every component reads variables, never the theme. Two fonts, no exceptions. Colors carry meaning only through tokens. Motion transforms and opacity only, nothing that costs layout. One accent; gold, moss, and clay are season notes, not decoration. Every control has designed hover, focus, active, loading, empty, and error states before it ships.

The full system lives in assets/css/main.css?v=1.0.2, organized in cascade layers so a future build can fork the tokens and keep the bones. That is the point of writing it down.
