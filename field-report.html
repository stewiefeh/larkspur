Kitchen board · Week of Oct 12

The Monday
field report

Eleven farms, one oyster bed, and what the walk-in knows by nine. The week's menu is written from this board. Read it before you book and you will know what dinner tastes like.

Posted Monday, Oct 12 · 8:47 am

This week

What is ready, thin, and done

Fishkill FarmsFishkill, NY

Apples, quince Ready

Empire and Stayman for the dessert, quince for the mostarda. Last cider pressing of the season lands Friday.

Grazin' AcresHillsdale, NY

Duck, whole Ready

Twelve birds, dry-aging in the cellar since Thursday. The long table gets four of them on the 22nd.

Sunspring FarmGhent, NY

Trout Thin

Cold snap slowed the ponds. The a la carte trout becomes a dish for two nights only, then goes until the water warms.

Meadowlark DairyAncram, NY

Cultured butter, smoked cream Ready

The herd moved to winter pasture; butter is nuttier this week. Smoked cream continues for the apple.

Chatham BedsAcross the river

Oysters Ready

Best week of the autumn. Cold water, firm meats, high salinity. Order them at the bar before dinner.

Blue Hill at Stone BarnsPocantico Hills, NY

Tomatoes Done

The last case arrived Saturday and did not survive the weekend. Parsnips take the second-movement slot from Wednesday.
